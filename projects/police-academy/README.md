# Police Academy Big Data Practical Training Platform

A delivered solution-design case study published under the personal portfolio?s Solution Consulting category.

Public entry: https://loganjiangca.github.io/projects/police-academy/

English is the default. Use `index.html?lang=en`, `index.html?lang=fr` or `index.html?lang=zh` for a specific language. Links retain the selected language through six pages: contribution and results, background and value, architecture, five modules, teaching workflows, and hardware/floor plans.

The finalized Chinese version is the source for the English and French content and layout. The project was delivered in September 2022 and accepted within three months. Its contract/payment amount is CNY 3,182,000; approximate CAD and USD values use the Bank of Canada?s October 2022 monthly averages. Future cooperation revenue remains a separate potential benefit.

This folder contains static presentation assets only. Original Word documents, editing prompts, local previews and development files are excluded. Supplied drawings retain their source geometry and dimensions; localized SVGs overlay translated labels. The current equipment list distinguishes project configuration updates from the original technical proposal.

No build or backend is required. Open `index.html` locally or use the public entry. Figures support enlargement/zoom and all five modules use four sequential steps.
