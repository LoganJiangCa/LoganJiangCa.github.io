Indicators and screening logic

① Data and baseline filters

Each stock uses the most recent 100 trading days of forward-adjusted daily bars.

Price decline: Current close ÷ close 20 days earlier − 1。

Always exclude ST stocks, delisting stocks, invalid prices, turnover >20%, and market caps below the chosen threshold. Strict mode also excludes trailing P/E ≤0 or unavailable P/E.

② Low volume and volume expansion

Peak-day volume: volume on the highest-price day in the last 60 days.

Low-volume setup: the previous 3-day average volume ≤1/3 of peak-day volume.

Low-volume launch: low-volume setup + bullish candle + daily gain ≥3% + volume ≥1.5× the previous 3-day average.

Volume-supported stabilization: after the 20-day decline meets the tier threshold, volume ≥ the previous 20-day average × the chosen multiplier, with a bullish candle or long lower shadow.

③ Turnover rate

Check the lowest turnover in the previous 10 days. A prior value below 1% followed by current turnover of 5%–10% counts as an auxiliary capital-activity signal.

Current turnover of 5%–10% without a prior value below 1% is displayed only and earns no auxiliary point.

>20%: excluded immediately from every tier.

④ Candlestick confirmation

Double/multiple pins: at least two long lower shadows in the last 12 days, lows within 1.5%, and a bullish latest candle.

Bullish engulfing: the latest bullish candle’s full high–low range covers the preceding 1–3 consecutive bearish candles.

Morning star: a large bearish candle + lower-volume doji + bullish candle recovering at least half of the first bearish body.

Single pin/doji: the following 2–3 days must hold the low, with confirmation by the latest bullish candle; unconfirmed patterns do not count.

⑤ MACD

Uses EMA12、EMA26、DEA9; 100 days of data provide indicator warm-up.

Bullish divergence: price retests or breaks the recent swing low over about 40 days, while DIF and the negative histogram are stronger than at that low.

Strong confirmation: after bullish divergence, DIF crosses above DEA and the MACD histogram moves from ≤0 to >0. Without this crossover, display “awaiting confirmation”; it cannot upgrade a tier by itself.

⑥ BIAS、RSI、BOLL

BIAS5：Current price ÷ MA5 − 1; a value below −10% triggers a signal. BIAS10 is displayed below −8%.

RSI uses a simple 14-day gain/loss calculation and the lowest value of the last 5 days: <20 is oversold; <10 is deeply oversold.

BOLL: 20-day middle band ±2 standard deviations; a signal requires 3 consecutive closes below their respective lower bands.

These are auxiliary oversold indicators and do not independently confirm a reversal launch.

⑦ Support, stop and resistance

Support: the nearest five-day swing low below the current price within the last 60 days; otherwise, the 20-day low.

Stop: Support ×98.5%。

Resistance 1: the nearest level above the current price among MA5, MA10, the BOLL middle band, the 50% point of the 60-day range, and the 20-day high.

Resistance 2: max(60-day high, resistance 1 ×1.03)。

⑧ Reward-to-risk ratio

Risk: Current price − stop price。

Reward: First resistance − current price。

Reward/risk: Reward ÷ risk. Compare with each tier’s configured threshold.

⑨ Tiering and scoring

Tier 1: Tier 1 decline and reward/risk thresholds met, with ≥2 strong confirmations.

Tier 2: Tier 2 decline and reward/risk thresholds met, with ≥1 confirmed signal.

Tier 3: Tier 3 decline and reward/risk thresholds met, with a confirmed signal or ≥2 auxiliary indicators. Watch only; no buying.

Scoring: 3 points per strong confirmation; 2 per confirmed single pin/doji; 1 per auxiliary signal such as low volume, turnover, BIAS, RSI, BOLL, or unconfirmed MACD divergence.

⑩ Strong confirmations

Bullish volume expansion after low volume; volume-supported stabilization after an oversold decline; double/multiple pins; bullish engulfing; morning star; MACD bullish divergence followed by a bullish crossover and positive histogram.

Confirmed single pins and dojis are standard confirmations, not 3-point strong confirmations.

⑪ External confirmations

Prominent broker seats on the trading leaderboard, broad sector gains, and stabilization or recovery in margin-financing balances require independent data sources.

This version does not fetch or score these three checks, avoiding false confirmation from incomplete data.

⑫ Result ordering

Sort by Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3, then by indicator score, then by estimated reward/risk when scores are tied.

If a historical cache lacks newer indicators, a message prompts you to run the scan again.
